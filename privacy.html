A little about privacy

You can play without an account. We don’t ask players for names, email addresses, or other contact details.

Optional usage statistics

When connected, we use Umami, a cookie-free analytics service, to estimate how many visitors use the site and which games they open. Reports include technical information such as page visits, browser and device type, and approximate location processed by the analytics provider. These counts estimate visitors; they do not identify individual people.

We send game names and game-open or restart events. Our tracking code doesn’t add persistent player IDs, and it removes query strings and fragments from the page addresses and referrers it reports. We respect your browser’s Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals.

You can block analytics and keep playing every game. For details about the analytics service, see Umami’s privacy policy.

Saved game preferences

The read-aloud voice and reading pace you choose, and your Desert Dino Dash best score, are saved in your browser on this device. Read-aloud uses your device’s speech service; some voices use an internet connection. Google Fonts may also be requested for the site’s typography.
