Privacy for the iOS app

The Anne’s Arcade iPhone and iPad app bundles its games on your device. It does not require an account, show ads, or send website analytics. There are no in-app purchases.

Your chosen read-aloud voice, reading pace, and Desert Dino Dash best score are saved only on this device. Removing the app removes that data. The developer does not receive these preferences or scores.

Read-aloud uses the device’s speech service. Availability and offline support depend on the voices installed on your device; some voices may need an internet connection. The app does not implement microphone recording or speech uploads.

This policy describes the iOS app. The separately hosted arcade website has its own privacy page and optional usage statistics.
