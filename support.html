Anne’s Arcade support

For help with the iPhone and iPad app, a parent or adult can report a problem or ask a question on GitHub. A GitHub account is needed to post.

Tell us the game name, app version, device model, iOS version, and what happened. Please do not include children’s names, contact information, or other private information: GitHub issues are public.

If read-aloud is silent, check your device’s media volume and installed speech voices. Try a different voice in the story’s voice selector.
